صلاة الاستخارة
Salat al-Istikhara — The Prayer of Guidance
Before engaging your mind, engage your Lord. Istikhara is not about receiving a dream or a sign — it is about submitting your will to Allah's wisdom and asking Him to ordain what is best for your deen, your dunya, and your akhira.
🕌 Perform two rak'ahs (non-obligatory) with the intention of Istikhara
🤲 Recite the du'a of Istikhara, filling in the matter you seek guidance on
💭 Then use your God-given 'aql (reason) — that is also part of worship
🔄 Repeat over days if needed. Look for shifts in your heart's inclination
Du'a al-Istikhara
Sahih al-Bukhari — Narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah ؓ
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَخِيرُكَ بِعِلْمِكَ، وَأَسْتَقْدِرُكَ بِقُدْرَتِكَ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ الْعَظِيمِ، فَإِنَّكَ تَقْدِرُ وَلَا أَقْدِرُ، وَتَعْلَمُ وَلَا أَعْلَمُ، وَأَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ [اذكر الأمر] خَيْرٌ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي، فَاقْدُرْهُ لِي وَيَسِّرْهُ لِي، ثُمَّ بَارِكْ لِي فِيهِ. وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تَعْلَمُ أَنَّ هَذَا الْأَمْرَ شَرٌّ لِي فِي دِينِي وَمَعَاشِي وَعَاقِبَةِ أَمْرِي، فَاصْرِفْهُ عَنِّي وَاصْرِفْنِي عَنْهُ، وَاقْدُرْ لِيَ الْخَيْرَ حَيْثُ كَانَ، ثُمَّ أَرْضِنِي بِهِ.
Transliteration
Allahumma inni astakhiruka bi-'ilmika, wa astaqdiruka bi-qudratika, wa as-aluka min fadlika al-'azim, fa-innaka taqdiru wa la aqdiru, wa ta'lamu wa la a'lamu, wa anta 'allamu al-ghuyub. Allahumma in kunta ta'lamu anna hadha al-amra [name the matter] khayrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa 'aqibati amri, faqdurhu li wa yassirhu li, thumma barik li fihi. Wa in kunta ta'lamu anna hadha al-amra sharrun li fi dini wa ma'ashi wa 'aqibati amri, fasrifhu 'anni wasrifni 'anhu, waqdur li al-khayra haythu kana, thumma ardini bihi.
Translation "O Allah, I seek Your guidance by Your knowledge, and I seek ability by Your power, and I ask of Your great bounty. For You have power and I do not, and You know and I do not, and You are the Knower of all unseen things. O Allah, if You know that this matter — [name it here] — is good for me in my religion, my livelihood, and the outcome of my affairs, then ordain it for me, make it easy for me, and bless me in it. And if You know that this matter is bad for me in my religion, my livelihood, and the outcome of my affairs, then turn it away from me and turn me away from it, and ordain for me whatever is good wherever it may be, and make me pleased with it."
📖 Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Tahajjud, Hadith 1166

          Decision Frameworks

          ⚖ Pros & Cons — Weighted
          Assign a weight (1–5) to each factor. Not all pros are equal.
          1
          List all benefits and harms, major and minor
          2
          Assign each a weight from 1 (trivial) to 5 (life-altering)
          3
          Sum the weights for each side
          4
          If pros total ≥ cons, it leans forward — but also consider reversibility
          📌 Islamic note: Factor weight for impact on your deen should always be ≥ 5
          🔮 10 / 10 / 10 Rule
          How will you feel in 10 minutes, 10 months, 10 years?
          1
          10 minutes after deciding: Relief? Regret? Excitement? Dread?
          2
          10 months later: What is your life like? Has this shaped you positively?
          3
          10 years later: Will you wish you had chosen differently? What do you hope for?
          📌 Add: What will your answer be on Yawm al-Qiyamah?
          🧠 Regret Minimization
          Jeff Bezos's framework — project forward to age 80.
          1
          Imagine yourself at age 80, looking back on this moment
          2
          Would you regret NOT taking this path more than taking it?
          3
          We regret inaction more than action — but test this honestly
          📌 Islamic addition: Regret on the Day of Judgment > regret at 80
          🌡 The Gut + Data Check
          When logic and intuition conflict, run this protocol.
          1
          Write: "My gut says ___". Then list why it says that
          2
          Gather 3 concrete facts that support or challenge your gut
          3
          If gut and data align → high confidence. If opposed → dig deeper
          📌 In Islam, the heart (qalb) often carries spiritual intuition — do not ignore it
          🚦 Worst Case Stress Test
          Assume everything goes wrong. Can you survive it?
          1
          Define the absolute worst realistic outcome
          2
          Ask: is this survivable, recoverable, or catastrophic?
          3
          If survivable → fear should not stop you. Plan mitigation
          4
          If catastrophic → only proceed with exceptional justification
          📌 "And whoever relies upon Allah — then He is sufficient for him" (65:3)
          📜 The Shura Principle
          Seek counsel from those of wisdom, not just agreement.
          1
          Identify 2–3 people who have: (a) experience, (b) good character, (c) no stake in your choice
          2
          Ask them: "What would you do? What am I not seeing?"
          3
          Distinguish between advice rooted in their fear vs. genuine wisdom
          📌 Quranic principle: "And consult them in the matter" (3:159)
